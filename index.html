The people behind Success Brand

Success Brand was founded in 2021 to break down the barriers that kept people from the mental health support they deserve. Therapy here is built to feel relatable, culturally grounded and accessible.

JK
Founder & CEOJohn Kennebrew, LPCLicensed Professional Counselor

John leads Success Brand as a multi-state mental health practice centered on community empowerment. He also oversees The Showcase Group, Success Brand's nonprofit arm supporting justice-involved youth.

Vision & mission

  • VisionMental wellness that is accessible, inclusive and transformative for everyone.
  • MissionCulturally resonant mental health services, with personalized care and a real commitment to each client's well-being.

Our journey

  1. 2021Success Brand is founded to make therapy relatable and accessible.
  2. GrowthA network of 30+ licensed therapists serving individuals, families and underserved communities online.
  3. TodayLeading the way with therapy in virtual reality, including VR sessions, EMDR and exposure therapy.
  4. NextExpanding access, because healing shouldn't be a luxury.
